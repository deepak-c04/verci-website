Aurafy doesn't need an account or cloud AI to show lyrics. For an uncached lyric lookup, it sends the track title, artist, album, and duration to LRCLIB. On Mac, artwork lookup may use Apple's iTunes Search. Cached lyrics stay on your device.

Feature assistants run only when you ask. Mac’s optional Auto Sync uses on-device speech recognition while enabled. Aurafy does not save or upload the captured audio or recognized words. The opt-in source prototype analyzes audio from an identified player process and its child processes using a local speech recognizer; it is not downloadable and still needs Windows device checks. Aurafy does not send assistant prompts to a cloud service.

Before you hit play

Quick answers.
No mystery.

Compatibility, lyrics, video, and the early access builds—in plain English.

Does Aurafy work with Spotify and Apple Music?+

On Mac, Aurafy reads playback from Spotify and Apple Music after you allow macOS Automation access. On Windows, it reads the active Windows media session; the player must publish that session for track details and controls to appear.

Will every song have lyrics, in every language?+

No lyric catalogue can promise every track and language. Aurafy looks up community supplied lyrics from LRCLIB. If a result is missing, you can import an LRC or text file you have. Mac also supports on-device transliteration where available and imported translations.

Do the lyrics always match the exact singing pace?+

Timing depends on the lyric file. Word-timed lyrics can follow more closely; line-only or plain-text lyrics may use estimated timing. On Mac, turn on Auto Sync from the menu bar or Settings → Lyrics to compare locally recognized words with the original lyrics. Timing adjusts after several confident matches. It requires Screen & System Audio Recording and Speech Recognition permissions plus a supported on-device language. Audio and recognized words are not saved or uploaded by Aurafy. An in-session fit remains usable while playback is within 30 seconds of its current alignment point; a playback jump resets that point. A valid saved fit for that track and lyric version can also be used. Otherwise, Aurafy uses the lyric timestamps. Singing and rap recognition can be less reliable. The current Windows download has manual Offset and Pace only. Windows Live Auto Sync is in development, not included in that download, and still needs device validation.

Why is video an option on Mac but not Windows?+

Aurafy does not receive a direct Spotify video stream. On Mac, Spotify video is an estimated crop of the visible Spotify window and requires Screen & System Audio Recording permission; it captures no audio and doesn’t save or upload frames. Apple Music video works only when the player exposes a local video file. Windows video is not implemented yet.

What does “early access” mean?+

These are early builds for trying and sharing feedback. The Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; Windows packages are unsigned and may show SmartScreen. Windows still needs launch and player-session checks on physical devices. Review each ZIP’s README before running it.

Does Aurafy need internet or run all the time?+

Playback visualization works with the supported player connection. New lyric or artwork lookups need an internet connection; cached lyrics can be shown offline. Feature assistants are on demand and stop when Aurafy closes or the computer powers off.
