Verci / Data & privacy

Your data,
plainly.

For the current early preview · Updated November 18, 2026

This page describes the current behavior of Verci’s website and desktop preview apps. It is a practical data-use explanation, not a promise that every third-party service has the same privacy practices.

The website

This static website does not set analytics cookies, run analytics scripts, use tracking pixels, or load third-party fonts. It has no account form or newsletter signup. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP address and request time in its server logs, according to that provider’s settings and policies.

Music playback and lyric lookups

Verci reads the current track and playback state from your music player after you grant the operating-system permission. Track information is used to display what is playing and look for matching lyrics. When lyrics are not already cached, Verci sends the track title, artist, album, and duration to LRCLIB for the lookup. Lyric coverage depends on that community-supplied catalogue. Cached lyrics remain on your device and can be shown offline.

Artwork, imported files, and translations

On Mac, when artwork is not available from the player, Verci may look up artwork through Apple’s iTunes Search using the title and artist. Imported LRC or text lyrics and translations are processed and stored locally by the app. Verci does not upload those files as part of its normal lyric display.

Optional video on Mac

Spotify video mode captures the visible Spotify window to show an estimated crop. The app asks for macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Verci disables audio capture; captured frames are processed in memory and are not saved or uploaded by the app. Spotify layout changes may make the crop inaccurate. Apple Music video is available only if the player exposes a local video file to Verci.

Feature assistants and local models

Feature assistants respond only when you ask. Verci uses built-in guidance when an on-device model is unavailable. On eligible Macs, Apple’s Foundation Models service may answer locally when the operating system and Apple Intelligence state allow it. The Windows 11 24H2 preview can optionally download a local model; the initial download requires internet and may be large. Verci does not send assistant prompts to a Verci cloud service.

Third-party services

LRCLIB receives track metadata for uncached lyric searches. Apple’s iTunes Search may receive a title and artist for artwork lookup on Mac. Optional Windows model setup may retrieve model files from Microsoft’s local AI runtime. These requests are initiated by the app for the described features; the services’ own handling is governed by their respective terms and privacy practices.

Preview limitations

These are early preview builds. The app may change, and this page should be updated when its data flows or service providers change. The Mac app is not notarized and Windows packages are unsigned. See each download’s README before opening it.
